01

Overview

Ink Splash is a single-player tapping game. You can play it start to finish without creating an account, entering your name, or giving us an email address — we don't ask for any of those, because the game doesn't need them.

The sections below cover the only two ways information moves anywhere near a network from this app: the advertising SDK that shows interstitial and rewarded video ads, and the optional leaderboard feature. Everything else the app remembers about you — your best score, your streak, your settings — stays on your device.

02

What we don't collect

Ink Splash never asks for, and has no code path to request:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files on your device
  • Any payment card details (purchases, if you make one, are handled entirely by Google Play — see §6)
03

Advertising — Google AdMob

Ink Splash shows ads between levels and as an optional way to continue a run (a short video you choose to watch). These are served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising SDK built into the app.

To select, deliver, measure, and protect against fraudulent ads, AdMob automatically collects information on its own — independent of anything Ink Splash's own code does:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and device identifiersFrequency capping, fraud prevention, ad measurement
IP address, general device & network infoServing an ad that works on your device, approximate region
Ad interaction data (views, clicks, completions)Billing advertisers correctly, reporting

This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure — Ink Splash's developer does not receive or store it. During testing and development builds, AdMob is configured to serve only Google's publicly documented test ads, which carry no real advertiser data.

Your controls: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads for every app, including this one.
04

Leaderboards — Google Play Games

Ink Splash includes optional support for Google Play Games leaderboards. If this feature is turned on in a future update and you choose to sign in, your score, level, and win streak are submitted to a leaderboard under your Google Play Games profile (display name and avatar) — never your name or email directly. Signing in is always your choice; the game is fully playable without it, and if you never sign in, nothing is submitted anywhere.

This feature is governed by Google's Privacy Policy when active.

05

Data stored on your device

Ink Splash keeps a small amount of data locally, in your device's private app storage — never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Your best score and current win streak
  • Whether sound is muted, and whether you've completed the tutorial
  • Daily challenge progress (the date you last played, and that day's result)
  • Whether you've bought the ad-free Supporter upgrade, and any continue credits — see §6

This is deleted automatically if you uninstall Ink Splash, or manually from Settings → Apps → Ink Splash → Storage → Clear storage.

06

Purchases

Ink Splash offers an optional one-time Supporter purchase that removes full-screen ads. Every part of that transaction — your payment method, billing address, purchase history — is handled directly by Google Play's billing system. We never see or store your payment details; the app only learns whether the purchase succeeded, so it can unlock the feature.

See Google Payments' Privacy Notice for how Google Play handles that information.

07

App permissions

Ink Splash requests exactly two Android permissions, both standard for any app that loads ads:

PermissionUsed for
INTERNETLoading ads and, if enabled, submitting leaderboard scores
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEChecking connectivity before attempting either of the above

Ink Splash does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other permission.

08

Children's privacy

Ink Splash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If that changes in a future version aimed at a younger audience, this policy — and the app's ad configuration — will be updated accordingly before that version ships.

09

Your choices

  • Ads: reset or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Leaderboards: simply don't sign in with Google Play Games — every other feature still works.
  • Everything stored locally: uninstall the app, or clear its storage from Android Settings, to remove it immediately.
